How it works

One small pause. A completely different habit.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is ScrollRot really free?
Yes — completely free, with no premium tier, subscription, or hidden upsell. It's a tool I wanted to exist, so I made it and gave it away.
Does it collect or sell my data?
No. ScrollRot has no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends your browsing anywhere. Everything — your settings, limits, and usage counts — stays on your device.
Which platforms does it work on?
YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Reels, and Snapchat Spotlight, on the desktop versions in Chrome and Firefox.
Will it slow down my browser?
No. It's lightweight and only runs on the supported sites — everywhere else it does nothing at all.
How is this different from just blocking the sites?
Hard blockers are easy to resent and easy to disable. ScrollRot keeps your access but adds friction and awareness, so you build the habit of choosing — and you can still fully block content during focus sessions when you want to.
Does it work on mobile?
It's a desktop browser extension for Chrome and Firefox today. Mobile browsers don't support extensions the same way, so phone support isn't available yet.
